# Authors

## Original author

**Arron Wang** ([@weAllHateMicrosoft](https://github.com/weAllHateMicrosoft)) — created classOS
and wrote all of it, 19 July – 26 August 2026: 205 commits, ~92,000 insertions across 263
files, before any other contributor joined.

That covers the whole of it: the curriculum design and all 57 lessons, the interactive step
player and its 17 step kinds, the compiler-gated authoring pipeline, the Java execution layer,
the mastery and evidence model, the AI tutor and its cost controls and kill switch, the
translation system, the design system, and the deployment.

Written with AI assistance (Claude), directed and reviewed commit by commit — see the
`Co-Authored-By` trailers throughout the history. The decisions were mine: what the course
teaches and in what order, CodeHS parity, that lessons show rather than explain, that technical
terms stay in English with a gloss, that AI needed a hard off switch, that student messaging had
to go, and every rejection of an approach that did not work.

## How to see who wrote what

The history is the record, and it is complete:

```bash
git shortlog -sne --all        # commits per author
git log --follow <file>        # the full story of any one file
```

Release **v1.0** is tagged at the point the project was handed over. Everything at or before
that tag is the work above.
