**Title:** Built a free, no-signup Java practice tool with interactive lessons instead of walls of text — want to break it

**Body:**

Made this over the past few weeks after noticing (in my own class, and in myself) that the students who struggle most with intro Java are exactly the ones who won't read a paragraph of explanation before trying something. So instead of "read this, then answer a question," every lesson is a sequence of do-first steps — press run and watch it happen, predict what a snippet prints before you see it, drag broken code back into place, that kind of thing. Captions only show up *after* you've acted, not before.

It's not tied to any school or class — no signup, no email, one click and you're in a lesson. That's on purpose, not a limitation: nothing to register means nothing to review, so it works regardless of what your school's tech policy allows.

**What's actually live right now** (being upfront — it's early): 3 lessons of intro Java (printing, variables/types, arithmetic expressions), fully interactive. More coming, but I'd rather have 3 good ones than 15 half-finished ones.
